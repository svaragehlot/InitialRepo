## What it covers
A Siege Media guide on getting more mileage out of content you've already made: repurposing to save
time, reach new audiences, and boost ROI. It runs through 16 concrete ways to repurpose, across
formats and channels.

## Tactics I want to remember (in my words)
- Turn high-performing blog posts into hub pages.
- Convert tutorials into email mini-courses.
- Transform posts into YouTube Shorts or Reels.
- Turn written thought leadership into podcasts, and long-form posts into X threads.
- Pull stats/quotes into branded quote graphics.
- Refresh and repost with an SEO twist: update old stats, tighten copy, re-optimize for a fresher
  keyword.
- Repurpose content specifically for LLMs and AI search.

## Why it's useful for the playbook
Producing AI-assisted content is only half the job; repurposing is how one strong piece becomes
many, which fits a small team trying to show up "everywhere" (search, social, AI answers) without
starting from scratch each time.

## One idea I'd test in a playbook
Take one strong pillar article and run it through a fixed repurposing checklist (hub page + 3 social
formats + 1 AI-search refresh) and track the extra reach per hour spent.
