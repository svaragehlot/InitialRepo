## What it covers
Aleyda argues the old organic-search measurement model (rankings, clicks, sessions) isn't enough on
its own for AI search, and lays out a three-layer way to measure it. The key idea: each layer hands
a hypothesis to the next, so three separate reports become one connected diagnostic.

## The 3 layers (in my words)
- Layer 1 - Presence: Is the brand actually showing up in AI answers, and how? Broken down by
  platform, prompt group, persona, product line, or market, so you can see where visibility is weak.
- Layer 2 - Readiness: Are you structurally set up to be surfaced? Instead of a blanket audit, you
  test the specific structural reasons behind the gaps Layer 1 found (e.g. positioning on
  comparison/review sites like G2 and Capterra).
- Layer 3 - Business Impact: Is the AI visibility creating value? The goal is an honest reporting
  model for budgeting and prioritization, not perfect attribution (don't over-claim).

## Key takeaways
- Measuring AI search is its own discipline now, separate from classic rankings.
- Diagnose before you optimize: find where you're invisible, then test why, then tie it to value.
- She shares a free "3-Layer AI Search Diagnostic & Measurement Matrix" (Google Sheet) in the post.

## One idea I'd test in a playbook
Run a mini Layer-1 check for one brand: pick 10-15 category prompts, see if/where the brand appears
across ChatGPT/Perplexity/Google AI, and turn each gap into a Layer-2 hypothesis.
