# The BEST Way to Use SEO Content Briefs in Today's Marketing Landscape

- Expert: Bernard Huang (Clearscope)
- Author: Bernard Huang, co-founder, Clearscope
- Type: Blog article
- Published: Oct 11, 2024
- URL: https://www.clearscope.io/blog/SEO-content-brief

## What it covers
Bernard makes the case that in an "automate everything" world, the briefing step is exactly the part
you should not skip. He shares how his team uses SEO content briefs in their workflow, and credits a
focus on information gain and "Ranch-Style SEO" for a 54% jump in organic traffic since Q1 of that
year, despite all the change in search.

## Key takeaways (in my words)
- The brief is where strategy happens; rushing or fully automating it is what produces generic,
  forgettable content.
- "Information gain" matters: content should add something new, not just repeat what already ranks.
- A strong brief balances efficiency with the slow, in-depth thinking that actually makes content
  rank and convert.
- This is the same theme that made me pick Bernard: AI can speed up production, but it can't fully
  understand your audience, their pain points, or your brand voice, the brief is where the human
  supplies that.

## One idea I'd test in a playbook
A lightweight brief template that forces an "information gain" line for every piece (what does this
add that the top results don't?) before any drafting starts.
