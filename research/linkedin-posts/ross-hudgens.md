### Post 1

- URL: https://www.linkedin.com/posts/rosshudgens_b2c-content-marketing-does-not-drive-many-activity-7472660098964959232-Hc4I?utm_source=share&utm_medium=member_desktop&rcm=ACoAADx3E_cBFoFGOCcm9RYQpTzBEQJaMdqKj5g
- Topic: The LLM Traffic Breakdown: B2B vs. B2C
- Date: 16 June 2026

B2C content marketing does not drive many direct LLM clicks in 2026.

That doesn't mean it can't have value for other things, but in our analysis of 61 brands' content-type traffic from LLMs, traffic to editorial sections for B2C brands was only 7%.

On the B2B side, it was a completely different story: editorial/blog sections were more than a quarter of the clicks.

The likely reason? In B2B, "BOFU content" is a thing. Alternative and "Best X" pages are more prolific and performative in a way that they aren't in B2C.

Those conversion-focused pages get cited more in LLMs, all things equal, and thus are more likely to get clicks as a byproduct, too.

And there's also the unlisted influence these have on homepage clicks.

In B2C, those same page types are often covered by affiliate sites or high-authority publishers, in a way that just isn't as prolific within business circles.

The strategy indication for B2C? That influence in LLMs comes from other factors like affiliate sites and product pages.

Helping better optimize those pages, increasing the authority of your site, and driving mentions within affiliate listings is how you drive LLM influence instead.

They have impact in B2B as well, just not at the same level. Each vertical has a different scale, and strategy should adjust accordingly for that difference.

### Post 2

- URL: https://www.linkedin.com/posts/rosshudgens_ai-traffic-converts-34-better-than-organic-activity-7470498245148934144-JQb4?utm_source=share&utm_medium=member_desktop&rcm=ACoAADx3E_cBFoFGOCcm9RYQpTzBEQJaMdqKj5g
- Topic: AI Search Conversion Benchmarks
- Date: 14 June 2026

AI traffic converts 34% better than organic on average.

In B2B, it's 49%. In B2C, a tamer 19%.

That's what we found reviewing 77 sites with statistically significant AI search traffic.

At the start of the LLM fervor, people were claiming AI converted 4x, 5x, even 7x better than traditional search. We saw a couple outliers in that range. But the wider majority sits in a much tighter band, closer to organic than the hype suggested.

That's still impressive. Organic is already a strongly targeted channel, and one that keeps improving as clicks decline thanks to AI features doing exactly what the LLMs do directly.

The takeaway for brands: compare these numbers to your own.

If your AI conversion rate is at par with organic, that's reason to dig in further.

A big reason B2B converts so much better than B2C is bottom-funnel transactional content. X vs Y, Y alternatives, best X software. Those comparison types are simply more prevalent in B2B.

If your conversion rate lags and you don't have that content, this is one more justification to build it.

And if you think AI search is an underwhelming channel for you, it may just be that you don't have the content to support the traffic, so it never feels like one.

A final point: if you can't even figure out these numbers, that's a bigger problem you need to fix now. Approximately one in four GA profiles we looked at didn't have goals setup at all.
