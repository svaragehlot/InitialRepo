- Topic: The Year I Couldn’t Work Was the Year My Channel Hit 300K
- Date: 19 June 2026

300,000 on YouTube today.

I sat with that number in gratitude this morning — because there was a version where it never happened.

In January 2025, my body crashed. Long COVID. Fifteen diagnoses, over a hundred symptoms, my heart slipping into AFib. I was 33, in the best health of my life — and three months later I couldn't sit at a desk or carry a single call. I thought I was dying. And I'd just launched First Movers.

So I leaned on the only thing I had left: a clone I'd built a month before the crash. Divine timing — I didn't plan it. Because I couldn't film, she filmed. Because I couldn't carry a ten-minute video, she carried it.

The first six months were brutal. The channel tanked. I prayed for the strength to go back to doing it the human way. But I couldn't. So we kept going — me grinding my teeth that control had left my hands, her sharing my thoughts, my content, day after day.

And then it moved. The comments changed. "Hey, it's Dr. McCoy. We love you, Bones." You named her before I understood what you were seeing. I'd bought the Star Trek costume because I love the mission of the Enterprise — never connecting there's a Dr. McCoy on that ship. You saw it before I did.
Here's the part that still makes me emotional: the biggest year of my career happened during the year I could not work. The dream I'd chased for thirteen years arrived the second I let go of the wheel.

God is in my story.

But the number isn't the story.

here’s what those three things mean together.
