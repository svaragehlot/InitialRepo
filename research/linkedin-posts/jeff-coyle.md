Community college marketers can be the Pivot Generation. That framing came from Austin Community College's session at TACCM this week, and it's one worth carrying forward.

The argument: the marketers working in community colleges right now are positioned to reshape how prospective students — and AI — understand what these institutions offer. One number from the session made that concrete: 18% of prospective students have already removed a college from their consideration list based on what AI surfaced about it. No campus visit. No admissions conversation. Just a query, an answer, and a decision made.

This is the territory where content quality and AI visibility converge. The institutions showing up clearly in AI search are the ones with structured, accurate, accessible content. The gap between those institutions and the ones without visibility into where they stand is widening.
